About me

I'm currently completing a BSc in Computer Science part-time at Unisa while building my way through full-stack JavaScript, one project at a time. Most of what I build starts as a question I couldn't answer, and ends as something I understand well enough to explain. I'm looking for a junior role where I'll write code every week and learn from people who've been doing this longer than I have.
