About me

I'm a Cape Town–based web developer with a background in pharmacy, where five years behind a dispensary counter taught me that precision and patience matter more than speed. I'm currently completing a BSc in Computer Science part-time at Unisa while building my way through full-stack JavaScript, one project at a time. I like the problems that don't resolve on the first attempt — the layout that breaks at 480px, the function that returns undefined for reasons that only make sense an hour later. Most of what I build starts as a question I couldn't answer, and ends as something I understand well enough to explain. I'm looking for a junior role where I'll write code every week and learn from people who've been doing this longer than I have.
